Employer obligations & risks without suitable health insurance

For companies with international teams, the employer's duty of care for overseas assignments is the key lever – international employee health insurance fulfils this responsibility in a structured way. Hallesche explicitly highlights duty of care under Section 17 SGB V and employer responsibility for overseas deployments.

01 Employer's duty of care for overseas assignments

For postings, inpats and international assignments, employers have a special responsibility for employees' healthcare. Under Section 17 SGB V, duty of care also applies during stays abroad – suitable international employee health insurance helps companies meet this obligation and gives HR and management legal certainty.

02 Risks without adequate cover

Without a structured solution, gaps appear: travel health insurance alone often does not cover long stays; high treatment costs abroad burden employees and companies; and in an emergency, assistance or medically appropriate repatriation may be missing. That creates financial, legal and reputational risks.

03 Day-to-day requirements: posting, inpat, business travel, workation

Each deployment model has different requirements: assignees need permanent worldwide cover, inpats need a solution in Germany, business travellers need flexible short-term cover concepts, and workation models need clear rules on length of stay and scope. A well-designed employee health insurance programme brings these scenarios together.

FAQ on international employee health insurance

Answers for HR, people teams and management on international employee health insurance – short and clear.

What is international employee health insurance?

International employee health insurance is employer-sponsored cover for staff with international assignments – from postings (expats) and inpats in Germany to business travel and workation. It goes beyond simple travel health insurance and supports the employer's duty of care.

Which employee groups is the solution suitable for?

For expats abroad, international specialists in Germany (inpats), global business travellers, workation and remote-work models, and international project teams. Scope is tailored to target group, countries and length of stay.

Is travel health insurance enough for postings?

For short business trips, travel health insurance may be enough. For longer postings, inpats or workation, companies usually need permanent international employee health insurance with broader benefits and clear employer responsibility.

What role does Hallesche health insurance play?

SpreeFinanz works with Hallesche health insurance (ALH Group). Plans offer worldwide cover, assistance, a 24-hour hotline, telemedicine and digital administration – and support meeting duty of care under Section 17 SGB V.

How does the free consultation work?

You complete the intake form or book a free consultation. We analyse your needs (deployment types, countries, headcount) and develop a suitable, non-binding cover concept for your international employees together.

What does duty of care mean for overseas assignments?

Employers must protect employees' health – including during stays abroad. Structured international employee health insurance helps meet this responsibility so staff are not left facing high costs or unclear benefits in an emergency.
