APRIL International: Expat and Travel Health Cover for Germans Abroad

APRIL International works with a modular building-block system: you combine benefit modules and deductibles so that you only pay for what you actually need – from pure emergency cover to comprehensive protection. For Germans abroad this is especially useful when your situation does not fit a standard pattern.

You set the crucial switch right at the start: fixed-term or open-ended. If you start out as a long-term traveller and emigrate later, say so early – a short-term plan cannot be extended indefinitely, and later medical underwriting may turn out differently from today.

Highlights at a glance

  • Modular system: benefit modules and deductibles freely combinable.
  • Open-ended expatriate cover with worldwide validity – designed for expats and long-term stays.
  • Digital service: online application, the Easy Claim app for submitting bills as photos, and telemedicine around the clock.
  • Zone model: your premium depends on your target region – including home cover from certain zones, so you are also protected during stays in Germany.

The two plan families compared

My Health International – open-ended, for expats and long stays

Comprehensive cover with medical underwriting, no time limit and worldwide validity. This is the solution when your stay has no fixed end date: emigration, open-ended work abroad or life as a digital nomad.

The underwriting is not a drawback but the price of predictability: the contract continues even if you fall ill later. That is precisely what short-term plans cannot do.

MyTempoCover – short-term cover up to one year

Travel plans without medical underwriting for stays abroad of up to twelve months. Suitable for a sabbatical, working holiday, a semester abroad or a longer trip with a clear return date.

What to watch when choosing modules

Zone and USA/Canada

The zone model determines your premium. North America is the most expensive category because of treatment costs. Be honest in your assessment: regular stopovers and business trips count too – extending afterwards is more work than choosing correctly from the start.

Deductible

A high deductible noticeably lowers the premium, but it must be payable from your own funds – especially abroad, where clinics often bill before treatment.

Home cover and return

Clarify in advance how long and under what conditions you are covered in Germany, and what happens if you return permanently. This question is the most common blind spot in expatriate plans – more on it at health insurance abroad.

Pre-existing conditions

My Health International involves medical underwriting. Declare everything in full; incomplete statements are the most common reason for reduced benefits in a claim.

Alternatives compared

Depending on your situation, other providers may suit better: Cigna Global for very high benefit requirements, PassportCard if you never want to pay up front, Foyer Global Health for open-ended contracts with direct billing, BDAE for emigrants who need a prospective entitlement, and Care Concept for young travellers and students.

Frequently asked questions about APRIL International abroad

What is the difference between My Health International and MyTempoCover?

My Health International is open-ended comprehensive cover with medical underwriting and worldwide validity – designed for expats and long stays without a fixed end date. MyTempoCover is a travel plan without underwriting for stays of up to twelve months.

How does the zone model work?

Your premium depends on the region where you are staying. North America is the most expensive zone because of high treatment costs. From certain zones, home cover is included so that you are also protected during stays in Germany.

Am I covered during home visits to Germany?

Home cover is provided from certain zones. How many days per year it applies and whether it also covers longer stays differs by plan and should be clarified before you apply – particularly if you return regularly.

Can I extend MyTempoCover if I stay longer?

Short-term plans are designed for a maximum of twelve months and cannot be continued indefinitely. If your stay is extended you need a follow-up solution, and with open-ended comprehensive cover that means fresh medical underwriting. Plan for this early.

How do I submit bills from abroad?

Through the Easy Claim app you photograph the bill and submit it digitally. Telemedicine is also available around the clock, which is the fastest first point of contact in regions with limited medical infrastructure.
