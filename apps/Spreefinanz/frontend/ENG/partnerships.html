Our partner companies at a glance

Which company fits depends mainly on three points: where your centre of life is, how long you will stay, and whether you need a certificate for the authorities. The overview below groups providers by purpose.

For Germans abroad

  • APRIL International – modular building-block system with a zone model. Open-ended and fixed-term APRIL International health insurance – modular cover with or without medical underwriting, depending on duration.
  • Foyer Global Health – Luxembourg premium provider with open-ended contracts, a lifelong coverage guarantee and direct billing with clinics. Tiers Essential, Special and Exclusive.
  • PassportCard – Mastercard-based payment card with real-time top-up. No upfront payment, no collecting receipts, German-speaking service around the clock.
  • BDAE – more than 25 years focused on emigrants: Expat Infinity without a term limit, Expat Smart for fixed-term stays, plus prospective entitlement and Expat Legal.
  • Cigna Global – very large worldwide provider network, selectable areas of cover including or excluding the USA, strong benefits in oncology and mental health.
  • Care Concept – affordable plans for study, au-pair, work & travel and long-term trips of up to five years.

For foreigners in Germany

  • Hallesche Hi.Germany – German comprehensive private health insurance for fixed-term residence permits of up to five years. Fully eligible for an employer subsidy, cancellable monthly, English-speaking service.
  • APRIL International, Care Concept, Foyer, Cigna, PassportCard and BDAE – each with the plan variants tailored to a stay in Germany.

For companies

  • HDI Corporate Travel – travel cover, assistance and crisis management for firms with internationally travelling or posted staff.
  • International employee health insurance – occupational cover for expats, inpats and workation models.

Network and memberships

Besides the insurance companies we work with law firms, tax advisers and relocation services. We have been a partner of the Bund der Auslandserwerbstätigen since 2002. More under Partners and networks.

Apply online or get advice first?

A direct online application makes sense when your situation is clear: a fixed-term stay, no pre-existing conditions, a known benefit need. Speak to us first if any of these apply:

  • You have pre-existing conditions or an ongoing treatment.
  • You need a certificate for an immigration office or a visa.
  • The USA or Canada are part of your area of cover.
  • You want to insure family members as well.
  • You had German private health insurance and plan to return – keyword: prospective entitlement.

In these cases how the application is completed decides whether cover actually pays later. That is not a detail – it is the core of the matter.

Frequently asked questions about applying online

Does applying through Spreefinanz cost me more?

No. Premiums are the same as if you applied directly. We are paid by the insurance companies as part of the brokerage. There is no surcharge for you, but you do get a named contact in a claim.

Am I still looked after after applying online?

Yes. The application routes on this page are personalised to Spreefinanz. That means you stay in our care even if you complete the contract yourself – for billing questions, changes or a claim.

When should I get advice first?

Whenever pre-existing conditions, a certificate for the authorities, USA cover, family members or a planned return to Germany are involved. In those cases how the application is completed decides whether cover actually pays when it matters.

How quickly do I get my insurance confirmation?

With complete information, many digital application routes issue confirmation within a few minutes. That matters especially if an appointment with the authorities or an embassy is already booked.

Can I send documents via WhatsApp?

Yes. Via WhatsApp you can send us messages and documents directly. In practice that is the fastest route, especially if you are in another time zone.

HDI Corporate Travel – brief introduction for Spreefinanz

HDI Corporate Travel is an integrated insurance solution for travellers abroad and expats, combining cover and assistance from a single source. The focus is comprehensive protection for medical, security and organisational risks on business trips, postings and longer stays abroad.

Besides international travel health and accident insurance, it includes extensive medical assistance, a 24/7 emergency hotline and access to the TRAVEL ASSIST mobility risk-management platform. That platform warns about country and security risks, travel advisories and visa requirements, and coordinates help in an emergency – worldwide and in several languages.

HDI Corporate Travel is aimed at companies, the self-employed, digital nomads and internationally active professionals who need professional, predictable cover for stays abroad. Travel to high-risk areas and supplementary cover for expats are also possible.

Cigna Global Health Insurance

Cigna Global Health Insurance (Cigna Global Health Options) is internationally oriented private health insurance for expats, emigrants, digital nomads and internationally active self-employed people. The insurer is among the world’s leading providers of international health insurance and offers flexible, modular cover with global validity.

Who it is for
